Chakraview Solutions

My journey at Chakraview Solutions began as a Data Analytics Intern during the summer break after the first year of my Master's degree. It was my first experience working in a professional environment, where I learned how businesses operate, how teams collaborate, and how data supports decision-making.

During my internship, I worked on analysing business data using Python, SQL, Power BI, and Tableau. I cleaned datasets, created dashboards, prepared reports, and generated insights that helped the team understand business performance and customer behaviour. This experience taught me not only technical skills but also how to communicate insights to non-technical stakeholders.

After successfully completing my internship, I was offered a part-time role, allowing me to continue working while pursuing my Master's degree. Balancing academics and client projects was challenging, but it became one of the most valuable learning experiences of my career.

During this period, my responsibilities expanded beyond analytics. I started contributing to MERN Stack web development, working on both frontend and backend features while continuing to build analytical reports and dashboards. I also explored how automation and AI tools could improve development speed and productivity.

Alongside my technical work, I mentored four interns. Guiding them through analytics concepts, project workflows, and day-to-day problem solving strengthened my own understanding and introduced me to leadership and collaboration.

I was also involved in supporting the marketing team by analysing campaign performance, preparing reports, and helping them make data-driven decisions. This exposure allowed me to understand how analytics influences different business functions beyond software development.

After Graduation

After completing my Master's degree, I was offered a full-time position as a Data Analyst & Full Stack Developer. With greater responsibility, I became involved in managing end-to-end project execution—from analysing business requirements and designing solutions to development, reporting, testing, and deployment.

My role combines data analytics and software development. I analyse business data to identify opportunities, create dashboards and MIS reports, automate repetitive processes, and build web applications that solve real business problems. I regularly use AI-assisted development tools to improve productivity, accelerate implementation, and deliver solutions more efficiently.

As my experience grew, I took ownership of coordinating project pipelines, collaborating with cross-functional teams, and ensuring work was delivered on time while maintaining quality standards.

One of the most rewarding parts of my journey has been mentoring people. Throughout my full-time role, I have mentored seven interns, many of whom were from Data Analytics backgrounds. Helping them learn SQL, Python, dashboard development, and practical business analysis has been as valuable for me as it has been for them, reinforcing both my technical knowledge and leadership skills.

Areas I Worked In

  • Data Analytics & Business Intelligence
  • Python, SQL, Power BI & Tableau Reporting
  • MERN Stack Web Development
  • CRM Analytics & MIS Reporting
  • Business Process Automation
  • AI-Assisted Software Development
  • Marketing Analytics
  • Project Coordination
  • Intern Mentorship & Team Collaboration

Looking back, my journey at Chakraview has been a gradual transition from learning the fundamentals as an intern to taking ownership of projects, mentoring team members, and contributing across analytics, software development, and business operations. Every stage brought new challenges, continuous learning, and opportunities to grow both technically and professionally.
